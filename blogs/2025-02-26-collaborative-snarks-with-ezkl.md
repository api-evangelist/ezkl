Since we started EZKL, some users have repeatedly asked the following: Alice owns a private model, Bob owns private data. How do they compute an EZKL proof of Alice’s model applied to Bob’s data while keeping both model and data confidential to their respective owners? Keeping both the model and the data private to their respective parties would be a significant breakthrough.
