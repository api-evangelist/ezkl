Introduction Verifiable computation is the notion of proving correctness and security between nodes in network systems. Proof-carrying data (PCD) is an approach to achieving verifiable computation through cryptographic mechanisms. PCD has been a topic of research for a little more than a decade — if you’re unfamiliar, we suggest checking out the appendix before we discuss our experiments in practically building PCD systems.
