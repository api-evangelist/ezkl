After initial breakthroughs during DeFi summer, that included the popularization of constant function AMMs like Uniswap or overcollateralized on-chain lending protocols like Aave, innovation and progress in DeFi has … slowed. Part of this slowdown stems from the peculiarities of on-chain finance: transaction latency from block times, slippage in trades stemming from MEV, and low liquidity environments that are ripe for exploits, all of which require the development of new financial theory.
